Fitness Tips

💪
Progressive Overload
Gradually increase weight or reps. This principle drives muscle growth and strength gains over time.
🥗
Nutrition Matters
Eat balanced meals with protein. Good nutrition fuels your workouts and aids recovery.
😴
Recovery is Key
Get 7-9 hours of sleep. Rest allows your muscles to repair and grow stronger.
💧
Stay Hydrated
Drink plenty of water. Hydration improves performance and aids muscle recovery.
🔄
Consistency
Work out regularly. Consistency is more important than intensity for long-term gains.
🧘
Proper Form
Focus on technique. Good form prevents injuries and maximizes muscle engagement.
