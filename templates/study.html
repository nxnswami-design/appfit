Study Tips

🎯
Set Clear Goals
Define what you want to learn before each session. This keeps you focused and motivated throughout.
⏰
Use Pomodoro
Work in 25-minute intervals with short breaks. This technique boosts productivity and prevents burnout.
📝
Take Notes
Write down key points during study. Active note-taking improves retention and understanding.
🚫
Eliminate Distractions
Turn off notifications and find a quiet space. A distraction-free environment is crucial for deep work.
😴
Rest Well
Take breaks every 25 minutes. Get enough sleep for better memory consolidation and focus.
🔄
Spaced Repetition
Review material at increasing intervals. This powerful technique locks knowledge into long-term memory.
